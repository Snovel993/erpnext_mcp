# v0.63.1 — the same drop, pointed the other way

One bug, in four places, and it is the mirror image of the one v0.62.0 fixed.

---

## What v0.62.0 fixed, and what it left standing

`farmops_api/routes.py` reduces every request body to the keys the answering
method's signature actually names. That filter is deliberate and is asserted as a
property of the surface: a `**kwargs` wrapper would forward a phone's whole body
into a tool, so none of them has one.

The cost of the filter is that an argument spelled the other way does not error —
**it vanishes**. v0.62.0 published three aliases for that reason rather than
renaming anything, because the handset spells two of these arguments differently
than the older methods do:

| The app posts | The older method declares |
|---|---|
| `assignable_only` | `include_full` — the inverse question, with the opposite default |
| `unit`, `assigned_date` | `housing_unit`, `check_in_date` |

What that release did not do is declare each door's own spelling **and the
other's**. So the drop it fixed for one direction stayed open in the other:

- `include_full` sent at `list_housing_units` vanished, and the caller got that
  door's wide default — including when it sent `include_full: false` and meant
  the open beds alone. A list of cabins nobody can be put in, with nothing on the
  wire saying so.
- `assignable_only` sent at `list_available_housing` vanished the same way.
- `housing_unit` / `check_in_date` sent at `create_housing_assignment`, and
  `unit` / `assigned_date` sent at `assign_housing`, arrived with the cabin and
  the date gone — a hire refused for want of a start date the body had carried,
  naming an argument the caller had never heard of.

The read failure is the worse of the two, and it is the one the v0.62.0 audit
named: a refused write is loud, and a wrong list of beds is quiet.

## What changed

All four methods now declare **both** spellings, reconciled in one place in
`api/mobile.py` — `_camp_breadth` for the filter, `_one_spelling` for the cabin
and the date.

**Neither door's default moved**, and that is the property to keep. The two names
are not synonyms; they are opposite senses with opposite defaults, because they
answer different questions:

| Body | `list_available_housing` | `list_housing_units` |
|---|---|---|
| neither spelling | open beds only | the whole camp, full and condemned marked |
| `include_full=true` / `assignable_only=false` | the whole camp | the whole camp |
| `include_full=false` / `assignable_only=true` | open beds only | open beds only |

Every handset already in an orchard sends neither spelling or exactly one of
them, so nothing in the field changes answer. `HousingAPI.listUnits` sends
`include_full` and one key only; `OnboardingHousing.apiParams` sends
`housing_unit` and `check_in_date` and one spelling only.

**A refusal quotes the spelling the body actually used.** `_house_one_person`
already took the argument's label so its refusals would read on the screen the
caller is looking at; the label now follows whichever of the two spellings
arrived, rather than whichever the door happens to prefer.

**A body that says both to contradictory effect is refused, not resolved.**
`include_full=true` beside `assignable_only=true` asked for the whole camp and
for the open beds in one breath, and `unit` beside a different `housing_unit`
names a cabin somebody is being put in and one they are not. There is no reading
of either that is truer than the other, so both are refused by name with the keys
quoted back, and nothing is read or written. No client this app knows about
produces such a body.

## What is still NOT aliased across, on purpose

`assign_housing` still declares neither `allow_multi_occupancy` nor `company`.
That method's promise is that it passes the barracks flag as true on the caller's
behalf **under** capacity and refuses **at** it, and a spelling alias is not the
place to hand a phone the one argument that changes that answer.
`create_housing_assignment` is the door that declares both, and it is the one the
hiring wizard posts to. `HousingAPI.assign` sends `allow_multi_occupancy` today
and says in its own comment that it travels and does nothing; that stays true.

## Tests

- `test_farmops_api.py` — both spellings of both pairs survive `bind` at all four
  doors, and the two arguments `assign_housing` deliberately refuses still do not.
- `test_ios_contract.py` — the narrow question asked four ways at two doors
  returns one list and the wide question likewise; neither default moved; a
  contradicting filter is refused by name; both spellings of the cabin and the
  date write the same assignment; a refusal quotes the spelling that was sent;
  two spellings naming two cabins is refused with nothing written.

No route was added, so nothing needs mounting on the funnel.
