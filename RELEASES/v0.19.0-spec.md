## Regulatory context (from tonight's research)

Training records live at the intersection of multiple regimes:
- **USDA GAP** — annual food safety training required for every worker who handles produce; retained 2+ years
- **USDA NOP** (via Oregon Tilth) — organic-specific training for anyone handling organic inputs; retained 5 years
- **WPS (40 CFR 170.401)** — pesticide worker training every 12 months, handler training more comprehensive; retained 2 years per §170.309
- **OR-OSHA** — heat illness prevention (`OAR 437-004-1131`) required annually before heat exposure; respiratory protection if applicators use respirators; hazard communication with actual pesticide labels + SDSs; retained 3-5 years
- **FSMA §112.22-.32** — PSA grower training required for at least one supervisor per farm; per-worker food safety training annual; retained 2 years

Each regime has its own retention window. The Employee Training Record doctype must carry `regimes` (multi-select) so the same training can satisfy multiple audits and the audit packet generator can pull the right records per audit.

## FSMA §112.161 universal fields

Every activity record (including training records) must carry:
- `person_performed_signature` (who was trained — captured at completion time, not backfilled)
- `supervisor_reviewed_by` + `supervisor_reviewed_on` + `supervisor_signature`
- `activity_datetime` (not date-only)
- `regimes` (multi-select)
- `content_topics_covered` (drives cross-audit reuse)

Bake these in from the start.

**Auto-status hook (before_save)**:
- `expires_date` is None → Active
- `expires_date` < today → Expired
- `expires_date` <= today + 90 days → Expiring
- Else → Active

## Compliance rule: `training_expiring`

Pattern after `certification_expiring` in `erpnext_mcp/alerts/rules.py`. Rule ID format: `training_expiring:Employee Training Record:<name>`.

- Severity **Critical** if Expired
- Severity **Critical** if within 30 days of expiration
- Severity **Warning** if within 90 days
- Alert body includes `regimes` so calendar shows "OSHA Heat Illness training for Ana expires in 12 days"

## MCP tools

All through the same guard pattern as `create_employee` (role gate HR Manager/Farm Manager/System Manager, company scope via Employee.company, kill switch, audit log, mutating default OFF).

- `record_training(employee, training_type, completed_date, regimes, content_topics_covered, expires_date=None, provider=None, certificate_file=None, notes=None, training_source="Internal", completed_time=None)` — mutating
- `list_trainings(employee=None, company=None, status=None, expiring_within_days=None, regime=None)` — read-only. `regime` filter lets audit packet generation pull "all NOP training records for last 5 years" cleanly.
- `get_training(name)` — read-only, full detail
- `sign_training_supervisor_review(name, supervisor)` — mutating; sets supervisor_reviewed_by + on + signature stub. Signature file uploaded via stage_file_chunk pipeline separately then referenced by docname.
