# v0.97.0 — the survey answered from the records, not from February

Wave 5 of `fafo_ios/SERVER_CHANGES.md` — items 18 to 22. Five schema additions,
no iOS half, one purpose: **generating the Columbia Gorge Fruit Growers annual
membership survey as a PDF from the farm's own records** rather than from
somebody's recollection in February.

Each of them is small. What makes them one release is that the survey is
answerable end to end only when all five are in.

---

## The one idea

**Two of the five looked already done, and both were wrong in the same
direction.** A column existed, at the wrong granularity or on the wrong record,
and answered a question that sounded like the one being asked.

```
  the survey asks              the column that exists        what it can answer
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  total acres certified   →    Crop.is_organic_certified  →  "is Gala organic"
                                                             (one flag, twenty
                                                              blocks, eight of
                                                              them certified)
  which counties do you   →    Parcel.county              →  "where do we own
  operate in                                                  ground" — not the
                                                              leased blocks, and
                                                              not by acre
```

Certification attaches to **ground**. Acreage is a fact about **blocks**. Both
existing columns are correct about their own subject and cannot be aggregated
into an answer about a different one — which is exactly the kind of gap that
gets closed as "already done" by whoever picks it up next. There is a negative
control in the test suite for each.

---

## What shipped

### 18 — organic certification, on the block

Four columns on **Field**, this app's own doctype:

| field | type | note |
|---|---|---|
| `organic_status` | Select | `Conventional` / `Transitional` / `Certified Organic` |
| `organic_certified` | Check | **derived**, read-only, rewritten on every save |
| `organic_cert_agency` | Data | Oregon Tilth, CCOF, WSDA |
| `transition_start_date` | Date | the NOP's thirty-six months run from here |

A **Select rather than a checkbox**, because a checkbox records the end state
and loses the three years of transition that lead to it — which is the part a
buyer and an inspector both ask about, and the part a crop-level flag cannot
represent at all.

`organic_certified` is derived in the controller and refused as an argument by
`create_field` and `update_field`. It is the same rule the boundary's centroid
and bounding box are under: a derived figure a person can edit independently is
a figure that will disagree with what it came from, and the one that is wrong is
always the one nobody edited.

**Blank is not Conventional.** A block nobody has answered for is listed in
`without_organic_status` rather than folded into the conventional acres —
otherwise an unanswered farm reports as a fully-answered conventional one.

`list_fields` gains `organic_status` and `organic_certified` filters, and
returns `organic_certified_acreage`, `organic_transitional_acreage` and
`acreage_by_organic_status`. **The survey line is now a filtered sum.**

Three contradictions are **warned about and not refused**, because each is a
state some block is genuinely in: an agency recorded on a Conventional block (a
block mid-application really does have a certifier and no certificate),
Transitional with no start date, Certified Organic with no agency.

### 19 — the direct-marketed share, computed where it can be

Two additions, and the release is deliberate about which is which:

- **`Customer.sales_channel`** — `Direct` / `Wholesale` / `Packer` /
  `Processor`, a Custom Field installed at migrate time. The channel is a
  property of **who is buying**, so it is asked once per customer instead of on
  every invoice line — which is how it would go unanswered. With it, "what
  percentage of each commodity do you direct-market" becomes direct revenue over
  total revenue per crop, read off Sales Invoice: a **computed** figure.
- **`Crop.pct_direct_marketed`** — a Percent somebody types, reported with
  `pct_direct_marketed_source` naming it as an assertion. The survey is due in
  February whether or not that year's invoices are clean, and a number labelled
  as typed beats a blank. **What it must never be is a number a report presents
  as computed.**

`list_customers` returns `by_sales_channel` **and** `without_sales_channel`, and
the second is the half that matters: a percentage computed over a
half-classified register is a partial answer wearing a whole one's clothes.
Nothing backfilled the channel and nothing will — whether a buyer is a farm
stand or a packer is a fact only the farm has.

### 20 — pest management, as a table

A **child table** on Company rather than a single Link, because on this site one
consultant is the exception. Orchard Meadow runs Apple, Pear and Sweet Cherry;
a farm with pome fruit and stone fruit commonly retains a different adviser for
each, and a single Link holds whichever was typed last while reading as the
whole answer.

`Pest Management Provider` carries `provider` (Link → Supplier — a consultant is
already a Supplier on these books), `commodity` (Link → Crop), `service_type`
and `license_number` (Oregon licenses pesticide consultants under ORS 634). **A
row with no commodity covers the whole operation** and says so, rather than
reading as an unanswered question.

Written through `update_company`, which **replaces the table wholesale and
validates the whole list before writing any of it** — a half-written table
leaves a company with some of its advisers and no way to tell which half went.
Read by `list_companies`.

### 21 — county, derived and never copied

Field links to Parcel; Parcel has `county`. **No county column was added to
Field, and that is the item.** A second copy can disagree with the first, and
the one that is wrong is always the one nobody edited when the assessor redrew a
line.

`list_fields` reads it through the parcel on every row, rolls acreage up as
`acreage_by_county`, lists `counties`, and takes a `county` filter resolved to
that county's parcels. A county this site holds no ground in is **refused with
the ones it does hold** — a filter that quietly matched nothing reads as "we
farm no ground there" and one that quietly matched everything reads as "we farm
all of it", and a typo means neither.

There is a test asserting the stored Field row has no `county` key. It fails if
somebody adds one.

### 22 — four Governance Document categories

`Succession Plan`, `Family History`, `Acreage History`, `EFU Enterprise`.

The archive was already the right home — it carries the amendment chain,
effective and execution dates, and marks what is currently operative, which is
exactly right for succession planning and acreage history where "what applied in
2031" is a real question. **What was missing was the vocabulary.** None of Part
3 had a home except `Other`, and a survey generator cannot query `Other` and get
anything but everything.

These four are narrative rather than legal instruments. The `supersedes` chain
still works on them, though a family history is *revised* rather than superseded
by a later instrument, so `operative` on that chain only reads well if somebody
keeps it tidy. The doctype's own help text says so.

## What an operator does about it

Run `bench --site <site> migrate`. It creates two Custom Fields —
`Customer.sales_channel` and `Company.pest_management_providers` — and migrates
the Field, Crop and Governance Document doctypes. Nothing is backfilled and
nothing existing changes meaning: every blank stays blank, and on all five
columns a blank means "nobody has answered", which is a different fact from any
of the answers.

Then, in whatever order suits:

- **Set `organic_status` on each block.** `list_fields` names the ones still
  missing one in `without_organic_status`.
- **Classify the customer register.** `list_customers` names the ones still
  missing a channel in `without_sales_channel`.
- **Record the pest management consultants**, one row per adviser per commodity,
  through `update_company`.
- **File Part 3 as governance documents** under the four new categories, so next
  February's generator reads them instead of asking.

## The tests

`tests_standalone/test_cgfg_survey.py` — 41 tests, one class per item plus two
negative controls that are the point of the module:

- `TheCropFlagCannotAnswerAcreage` sets up three blocks of one crop in three
  organic states, asserts nothing about the crop distinguishes them, and then
  asserts the block-level sum does.
- `CountyIsNotCopiedOntoTheBlock` asserts the stored Field row has no `county`
  key, and that moving a parcel's county moves every block on it — which is what
  a read-through buys that a copy does not.

Each was checked by mutation: breaking the derivation, the read-through or the
duplicate-provider guard fails these tests and nothing else.
